SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 8. RACING CENTERS, TRACKS, RIVALS

PURPOSE
Separate race levels: seed-generated circuits and straights, rated AI rivals
calibrated to their tracks, and the five mainland racing centers with 10 races each.
Reference: track_gen.py, drag_race.py, racers.py.

HOW IT WORKS
- Track generation (track_gen): a circuit is the outline of a random blob of grid
  cells (6 tiles each), grown with no holes and no corner-only contacts, so the
  outline is a simple clockwise loop; parallel stretches stay 6 tiles apart. Start
  on the longest eastbound straight (6+ tiles). Same seed, same track; built on load.
- TrackLevel: 3-tile-wide track of the region's surface (track_<region>_base, _edge,
  _corner, _inner, _start, _finish), barrier walls (track_fence) and tire stacks
  (track_tires), ground drawn 10 tiles around; runoff drives with OFF_SURFACE.
  Straight: 63 tiles (4,032 px, a quarter mile). It implements the Level interface
  so the car works unchanged.
- Rival (AI): follows a precomputed racing line for all laps; cuts half the corners
  (seeded) to an apex 84 px inside the centerline and runs a little wide at the
  rest; brakes for corners; reacts 0.2-0.6 s late; on ice steers softer and drifts
  off the line (up to 72 px) then recovers. Rivals are non-solid to the player
  (ghosts); walls still stop the player.
- Rating: a rival rated R drives like a car of rating R. Calibration: simulate the
  rival once, then pick its corner speed so its lap equals a flawless lap by a
  player rated R-10 x CLEAN_LAP 1.04 (a clean lap just beats it 10 below). Then
  STRAIGHT_BOOST 1.02 x top speed on circuits (not on quarter miles). An off day can
  cost it up to 10 rating.
- Racing centers: 10 races per region, 3 laps, rivals rated
  100, 110, 120, 140, 150, 170, 220, 260, 300, 350 (race 10 = champion).
  The 50 center layouts (and the 5 island race layouts) are the exact tracks from
  today's game, loaded from the fixed map export (tracks/*.json, see 2-world).
  track_gen is still ported for tracks made at runtime: mission drag circuits and
  tournament tracks. Each region has a story and 10 named rivals with one line of
  banter (racers.py STORIES / RIVALS; keep the text).
- Flow: on foot within 130 px of a center, "E  Racing center · Race k/10" -> offer
  (rival line, "<Rival> (R) VS You (rating)", "3 laps on <surface> · win to unlock
  race k+1"). Win records the race, unlocks the next, autosaves; loss can be
  retried. Either way you return on foot at the center with the car nearby. No
  mastery from races. Completing all 10 = region champion. Blocked during missions.
- Race HUD: VS <RIVAL>, place, Lap n/3, time, metres to go; countdown banner.

ASSETS
track-atlas (all track_* sets incl. island, track_fence, track_tires), vehicle-atlas
racer_* (cyan, yellow, purple, orange, lime, blue, black) for rivals, prop-atlas
race_start / race_finish / race_checkpoint / barrier / cone, terrain-atlas ground.

CONNECTIVITY
- Used by 7-missions (drag races) and 15-elite-island (island races and 8-car
  tournaments reuse TrackLevel, Rival, calibration).
- Needs 1-car, 6-progression (ratings, races won, speed_scale), 5-ui (offer, HUD,
  banner), 2-world (center positions).

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/race/track_gen.gd (pure), scenes/race/TrackLevel.tscn built from a
  layout (TileMapLayer + barriers), scripts/race/rival.gd, scenes/race/Race.tscn
  (countdown, lap/progress along the centerline, finish), scripts/race/calibrate.gd
  (runs a fixed-step headless simulation; cache the result per track seed).
- Scene switching: the world scene is kept (paused/hidden) while a race scene runs,
  then restored.

DONE WHEN
- The imported 50 center layouts load and match the export; runtime-generated
  layouts are simple, clockwise, start eastbound, and barriers never sit on the
  track.
- For every center race, a scripted flawless player rated R-2 wins and R-12 never
  does (the Python test bounds).
- Rivals never leave the track; the player passes through them.
- Race load (including calibration) stays well under the 3 s countdown.
- Wired into the game flow as per plan 16 (interactions, prompts, mission locks, cleanup).
