SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 15. ELITE ISLAND

PURPOSE
The late game (a surprise until it opens): the ferry, the island racing center, 8
clubs in 4 leagues, 8-car team tournaments, top-league badges, and the spawn choice.
Reference: island.py, grid_race.py, fast_travel.py, main.py (ferry, clubs,
tournaments, _offer_spawn).

HOW IT WORKS
- Unlock (never hinted in any text before it happens): every mainland center
  10/10 and a region at level 25. The last center win then says "Elite Island is
  open!" and the "Island ferry" map diamond appears.
- Ferry: a kiosk beside the mainland ferry dock (sector 47,31), the ferry moored
  there, and a kiosk on the island dock. Boarding needs an Island pass (100,000 S):
  BOARD uses it up, the island stays open for good, and the crossing is instant, car
  and all. After that the ferry is free both ways, and T on a mainland beach travels
  there (T on the island returns). Island map marks show only after the crossing.
- On the island the car drives at the best region's speed (island clay surface).
- Island Racing Center (a 16 x 10 interior): three desks (Races, Clubs,
  Tournaments), trophy cases, podium, visitors.
  - Races desk: 5 races, 3 laps on island clay (track-atlas island set), rivals
    Coral Jax 360, Mako Senn 390, Lani Kade 450, Riptide Rue 500, The Admiral 550.
    Winning race 1 opens the clubs.
  - Clubs desk: offers only leagues above yours whose join rating you meet; then the
    two clubs; then JOIN (pays the entry fee). No leaving, never down.
      League 1 (360-400): Palm Runners / Reef Raiders, join after race 1, 20,000 S
      League 2 (410-480): Lagoon Kings / Coral Crew, rating 410, 40,000 S
      League 3 (490-590): Volcano Vipers / Tidebreakers, rating 490, 80,000 S
      League 4 (600+): Summit Aces / Sunside Elite, rating 600, 150,000 S
    Rating = the best region's rating. Each club has a camp on the island (race
    tent, flag, two club cars, members); E there gives league and rival.
  - Tournaments desk: BUY PASS (tourney_pass, 2,000 S) / START (uses one).
- Tournament: you and 3 teammates vs the other club in your league's 4; 3 races on
  3 different region surfaces (generated circuits, size 5-9, 2 laps each); the player
  drives at that region's speed.
  - AI ratings: offsets rolled once per tournament, added to the player's rating in
    each track's region. Teammates one -5..0, one +1..+5, one +3..+8. Team edge
    -5..+5; two rivals rolled (-5..0, +3..+8), the other two split the rest so the
    team averages differ by exactly the edge.
  - Grid: 8 cars, 2 lanes x 4 rows like a chessboard (home squares 0,3,4,7; away
    1,2,5,6); everyone, the player included, is shuffled onto their team's squares
    each race.
  - All rivals are ghosts (no car collisions); tournament racers slide more on ice
    (steer 1.6, recover 0.6, room 84). Rivals get STRAIGHT_BOOST like everywhere.
  - Points by place 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1; a race ends when the player finishes or all 7
    AIs finish; a quit puts you last. Standings popup after each race (NEXT RACE).
    After 3 races the higher total wins; a tie goes to the team whose best finisher
    placed higher in the last race.
  - Win pays a quarter of the league pool: 10,000 x 2^(league-1) S (pools 40k /
    80k / 160k / 320k) plus mastery 50 / 100 / 150 for leagues 1-3. League 4 pays
    tokens only; its wins count toward badges instead (secret until earned).
  - Race HUD: place of 8, lap, team score, "TOURNAMENT n/3".
- Badges (league 4 wins): bronze 10, silver 25, gold 50, green 75, blue 100,
  purple 150. A win that earns one says "<Badge> badge earned!" and nothing
  otherwise. Shown on the home badge board (11-home-and-arcade).
- Spawn choice: once in a club, opening the game and GO HOME ask CITY HOME / CLUB
  CAMP (the car waits in the camp's lot).
- Saved as "island": crossed, races, club, played, won, elite_wins.

  it opens.
- Join rules (race 1, rating floors, only upward, fees) hold.
- Over many tournaments, the team edge averages exactly as rolled; every car starts
  on its team's square; scoring and the tie rule are right; payouts per league.
- League 4 wins increment badges at 10/25/50/75/100/150 and pay no mastery.
