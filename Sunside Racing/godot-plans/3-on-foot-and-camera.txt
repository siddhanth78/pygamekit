SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 3. ON FOOT AND CAMERA

PURPOSE
Getting out of the car and walking, calling the car, and the camera (follow, zoom
between driving and walking). Reference: walker.py, main.py (camera_position, zoom,
_step_out, call), collision_manager.nearest_clear_spot.

HOW IT WORKS
- E beside a nearly stopped car (under 15 px/s) steps out: driver's side first, else
  passenger, back, front (EXIT_GAP 30 px). E within 44 px of the car gets back in.
- Walking: W/A/S/D or arrows move in screen directions at 60 px/s; Shift runs at
  105. 12 px square collision box; slides along walls. Walk cycle: people-atlas
  player_idle / player_walk_a / player_walk_b, a frame every 12 px walked.
  Poses: player_sit, player_lie (home, rides), player_fish_* (fishing).
- While on foot the parked car is a solid obstacle; traffic stops for it and for
  the walker.
- Q (call car): once the car is over 320 px away ("Q  Call car" prompt shows), it is
  placed at the nearest clear spot beside the walker (80-320 px away), facing the
  walker's way.
- R unsticks the walker the same way the car respawns.
- Camera: follows the player, clamped to the world/level edges, rounded to whole
  screen pixels. Zoom 1.0 driving, 2.0 walking; eases over 0.3 s (ZOOM_TIME) when
  getting in/out; snaps on travel, races, loading. Fair rides set their own zoom
  (ferris 0.9, carousel 1.5, bumper cars 1.4).
- Bottom-center prompt ("E  Get in", "Q  Call car", ...) comes from whatever is
  nearest (see 5-ui).

ASSETS
people-atlas: player_idle, player_walk_a, player_walk_b, player_sit, player_lie
(fishing poses in 9-fishing).

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 1-car (car pose, obstacle), 2-world / interiors (can_place_walker).
- Every interior level (home, store, farmhouse, fair, factory, island center)
  reuses this walker and camera with its own bounds.
- Feeds 5-ui (prompt), 7-missions (talk range to givers).

GODOT BUILD
- scenes/player/Walker.tscn (Node2D + Sprite2D, walker.gd).
- A single Camera2D owned by the game scene; script camera.gd with follow target,
  limits from the active level, zoom easing, pixel rounding.
- A PlayerController (game scene script) that switches drive <-> walk and routes
  input.

DONE WHEN
- Stop, E: the player appears beside the car; the car becomes solid; zoom eases
  to 2x. E near the car: back in, zoom 1x.
- Walking into scenery slides along it; Shift runs.
- Walk 400 px away, Q: the car arrives beside you facing your way.
- Camera never shows outside the world or a level and never shimmers.

PROMPT
"Add on-foot play to the Godot port per godot-plans/3-on-foot-and-camera.txt: step out
of a stopped car (side priority), walk with screen-direction movement at 60/105 px/s
with a 12 px box and wall sliding, 3-frame walk animation from people-atlas, the
parked car as an obstacle, Q to call the car to a clear spot 80-320 px away, R to
unstick, and a Camera2D that follows, clamps to level bounds, rounds to whole
pixels, and eases between 1x (driving) and 2x (walking) over 0.3 s. Tests for
exit/enter, call range, and zoom."

AS BUILT (2026-09-28)
- scripts/player/walker.gd (class Walker, scene scenes/player/walker.tscn: Node2D +
  Sprite2D "Sprite", z_index 130 = people band). x, y (64-bit floats; Vector2 is too
  coarse at world coordinates), heading (sprite turns with it), speed, stride, pose
  (held frame such as "player_sit"; "" = walk cycle). update(delta, move_x, move_y,
  run, collisions) uses collisions.can_walk (piers) when present. frame(),
  collision_box(), obstacle(padding), can_enter(car), place(x, y, heading),
  respawn_nearby(collisions) (8 px clearance). Static exit_spot(car, collisions)
  (driver, passenger 30 px; back, front 40 px; checked with can_move, as in Python)
  and call_spot(walker, car, collisions) (none under 80 px; nearest clear spot within
  320 px with the walker padded 16 px and 12 px clearance; sets car.heading to the
  walker's on success, restores it otherwise).
- scripts/player/game_camera.gd (class GameCamera extends Camera2D): bounds (level
  size), ease_to(zoom), snap_zoom(zoom), follow(target, delta) once per frame after the
  player moves; static camera_position (clamped top-left on whole screen pixels) and
  eased_zoom (out-cubic, 0.3 s, lands exactly). Fair rides call ease_to(RIDE_ZOOM).
- scripts/player/player_controller.gd (class PlayerController): car, walker, camera
  (exported); setup(level, collisions, bounds); tick(delta, move, run) (move in screen
  directions; driving uses -move.y as throttle, move.x as steer); interact() (step
  out under 15 px/s / get in within 44 px), call_car(), respawn(), prompt() ("E   Get
  in", "Q   Call car" past 320 px, else ""), speed_scale callable (surface -> scale,
  for 6-progression), mode_changed(walking) signal. Walking = walker.visible. While on
  foot collisions.fixed = [car.obstacle()]; getting in clears it.
- Dev scenes now use it: scenes/levels/car_playground.tscn (test level) and
  scenes/game.tscn (was scenes/world/world_drive.tscn, replaced by the game scene in 16) (world, from home).
- Traffic stopping for the parked car and the walker is 4-traffic-and-people.
- Tests: tests/unit/test_walker.gd, tests/unit/test_player_controller.gd.
