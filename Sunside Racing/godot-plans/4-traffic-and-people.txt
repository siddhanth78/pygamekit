SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 4. TRAFFIC AND PEOPLE

PURPOSE
Life in the world: traffic cars on fixed lanes, parked commuters, and pedestrians
(city, farms, camps, plazas, beaches, country roads). Reference: traffic.py,
parking.py, pedestrians.py.

HOW IT WORKS
All routes, stalls, and pedestrian groups come from the fixed map export
(world/traffic.json, see 2-world): they are data, not generated. What follows is
how they behave.
Traffic (right-hand lanes):
- City: rectangular loops on the road grid, 3 cars each (10 base loops + 70 extra
  loops: busy), 140-180 px/s; pairs of taxis, buses, police, delivery vans on their
  own loops. City lane 10 px from the road center (roads 42 px wide).
- Snow: 4 cars there-and-back on the ice road (100-120 px/s) + 2 SUVs.
- Rural: 4 on the main dirt road + 1 per branch (120-140 px/s), lane 7 px.
- Jungle, desert: 3 off-road 4x4s each on wandering loops (85-110 px/s).
- Highway: 3 cars per direction, 185-215 px/s, 40 px from the median.
- Cars: 60 px sprite, solid 20 x 40 (bus 22 x 50); turn toward their lane at
  360 deg/s; follow gap 56 px; stop short of the player/walker/pedestrians on the
  road and queue behind a car ahead. Intersection boxes (60 x 60): wait if a
  crossing car is inside or claimed it first; give up after 4 s (PATIENCE) so there
  is no gridlock. Yielding checks only within 1600 px of the player.
- Sprites: CITY_CARS (traffic_red/blue/green/white/gray/yellow/compact/wagon...),
  RURAL_CARS (pickup, service, wagon, gray), SNOW_CARS (suv, van, pickup, white),
  OFFROAD_CARS (pickup, suv, service), plus taxi, bus, police, delivery.
Parked commuters (city lots within 2 sectors of the player):
- 35% of nose-in parked cars (stable per stall) are commuters: after 3-25 s (then
  15-45 s between trips) one backs out, drives to the nearest north-south road,
  laps one block (115 px/s), and parks in the same stall. While out, its stall is
  empty; the moving car is solid and yields like traffic.
Pedestrians (groups within 2 sectors simulated, dropped past 3):
- City: 12 per block on the sidewalks (36 px from the road center), pausing at
  corners (0.8-3 s), entering building doors (hidden 3-9 s), crossing at crosswalks.
- Farms: 2 farmers circle each farm and use the barn door. Plazas: 3-4 people
  (city 4) mill around each racing center. Camps: 2 on the bench facing the fire, 2
  walking the ring and ducking into tents. Beaches: loungers under umbrellas, on
  chairs or mats (people-atlas beach_*_lounge / _lie), occasional strollers.
- Always simulated: 8 snow and 8 rural walkers on road shoulders.
- Walk 26-40 px/s, 32 px sprite, 10 px solid; they wait if the player stands 20 px
  in front; solid to the player except when already overlapping.

ASSETS
vehicle-atlas traffic_* (all), people-atlas city_a..h, farmer_a/b, snow_a/b,
nomad_a/b, explorer_a/b, beach_a..d (idle/walk/lounge/lie), *_sit poses;
canopy-atlas umbrellas; prop-atlas beach chairs/mats/umbrella_shade.

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 2-world (exported routes, lanes, stalls, doors, camps, beaches) and the
  player pose.
- Provides obstacles to 1-car / 3-on-foot, "people on road" blocking to traffic.
- Other systems reuse these movers: fair visitor cars and walkers (13), store
  shoppers (10), island camp members (15).

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/traffic/traffic.gd: all cars as plain data (arrays/dicts), updated in one
  loop; draw with a MultiMeshInstance2D per atlas or pooled Sprite2Ds for the ones
  near the camera. Avoid one Node per car (hundreds of cars).
- scripts/traffic/parking.gd, scripts/people/pedestrians.gd: group creation and
  removal by distance (hysteresis), same data-oriented approach.

DONE WHEN
- City start: about 18 cars on screen on average (Python measures 17.8 with the 70
  extra loops; the old "about 9" predates them), queues form behind a stopped
  player, cars never drive through each other in a queue, no car stalls > 10 s over
  several simulated minutes.
- Commuters back out, lap a block, and re-park in the same stall.
- Pedestrians walk sidewalks, cross at crosswalks, go in and out of doors, and
  traffic stops for them on crosswalks.
- Traffic + people update in about 1 ms per frame.

PROMPT
"Port Sunside Racing's traffic, parked commuters, and pedestrians (traffic.py,
parking.py, pedestrians.py) to Godot 4 per godot-plans/4-traffic-and-people.txt.
Keep them data-oriented (arrays updated in one loop, batched drawing), with the
listed routes, counts, speeds, yielding and intersection rules (4 s patience), the
commuter trips, and distance-based pedestrian groups for city blocks, farms,
plazas, camps, beaches, and country roads. Add GUT tests for lanes staying on
roads, queueing, no gridlock, and commuters returning to their stall."

AS BUILT (2026-09-28)
- scripts/traffic/traffic.gd (class Traffic, RefCounted): every car as parallel packed
  arrays (ids, names, path_of, speed, size, solid_w/h, reverse_first, trip_length, x,
  y, heading, segment, progress, travelled, waited), paths as flat
  PackedFloat64Arrays. Loads the 272 cars from world/data/traffic.json at their
  exported start. update(delta, player_box, blockers) is traffic.update: active cars
  within 1600 px; stop for any stopper whose box lies 18 px ahead (never while already
  overlapping); queue behind same-direction cars 0-56 px ahead within 16 px; crossing
  waits with 4 s PATIENCE. Queue and stopper checks use 64 / 128 px grids (same
  answers as the all-pairs Python loops, far cheaper). add_path_car, remove,
  index_of, box(i, ahead), nearby_obstacles, draw(pool, view); static lane_path,
  there_and_back, crossing_at.
- scripts/traffic/parking.gd (class Parking): the 103 commuters (stall, name, trip)
  from traffic.json; timers only for lots within 2 sectors; depart() adds a trip car
  (115 px/s, size 56, solid 18 x 38, first segment reversed); is_away(box) for
  Collisions.parking; hides/shows the stall sprite via World.set_stall_hidden.
- scripts/people/pedestrians.gd (class Pedestrians): people as parallel arrays; groups
  from world/data/pedestrians.json switched on within 2 sectors (at their exported
  start) and dropped past 3; 16 road walkers always. update(delta, player_box),
  nearby_obstacles, road_blockers, draw. Difference: Python regenerates a beach group
  away from the player; the fixed map can't, so a beach group waits to appear until
  the player is 110 px clear of its gear and people.
- scripts/traffic/sprite_pool.gd (SpritePool): reused Sprite2Ds, begin/add/end.
- scripts/traffic/world_life.gd (WorldLife node): setup(world, collisions) makes
  traffic and people solid (collisions.sources) and adds the parking filter;
  step(delta, player_box, blockers, view) runs parking, people, traffic (stopping for
  road_blockers too), then draws. Wired into scenes/game.tscn (was scenes/world/world_drive.tscn, replaced by the game scene in 16); the game
  scene (16) does the same.
- Collisions gained `parking` (skip boxes whose parked car is away); World gained
  set_stall_hidden / is_stall_hidden.
- Measured: traffic + 320 people 1.1 ms per frame at the city start (0.6-0.8 ms of it
  traffic); longest stall over 3 simulated minutes 1.3 s; 5-car queue behind a
  stopped player; 17.8 cars on screen on average (Python 17.8).
- Tests: tests/unit/test_traffic.gd, test_parking.gd, test_pedestrians.gd.
