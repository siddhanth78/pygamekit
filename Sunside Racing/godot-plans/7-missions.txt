SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 7. MISSIONS

PURPOSE
Mission givers and their three mission types (delivery, time trial, drag race), the
only source of region mastery. Reference: missions.py, drag_race.py (drag levels and
rivals), mission_ui.py.

HOW IT WORKS
- Givers: 3 per mainland region, one per type, about 200 px from the region's
  racing center; veterans (unlocked at region level 5) are extra copies of each
  type farther out; they double the reward. Positions come from the fixed map
  export (world/people.json); delivery recipients and time-trial checkpoints are
  rolled at runtime among drivable spots at the listed distances.
  Floating marker badges over each giver (marker-atlas icon_delivery / icon_speed /
  icon_drag, *_hard for veterans). Locked veterans still show their badge and
  explain the level needed.
- Talk (E within 40 px) -> offer panel (title, difficulty, detail, rules, reward)
  -> ACCEPT / DECLINE. Only one mission at a time. Offers are seeded and persist per
  giver until completed; a decline eases the next offer.
- Difficulty scale s in 0.5-1.25 (veterans 0.8-1.25); labels Easy < 0.8 <= Medium <
  1.05 <= Hard. Drag rivals are rated relative to the player (-10..+15; straights
  -10..+10; veterans +1..+10; after a decline -10..0).
- Delivery: take a package to a recipient 3,000-15,000 px away (often another
  region). No time limit. Start at 100 points; each crash costs 5/10/15 by
  difficulty; below 50 fails at once. 75+ pays 2, 50+ pays 1 (before level bonus).
  Deliver within 64 px.
- Time trial: reach a checkpoint 2,000-6,000 px away before the clock runs out.
  Limit = straight distance x 1.3 / (region top speed x level scale x 0.8 x s). The
  clock starts when you drive off. Finish within 90 px. Pays 1.
- Drag race: loads its own level (a 4,032 px quarter-mile straight or a generated
  single-lap circuit, themed to the region) against one rated rival; 3 s countdown;
  first to finish wins. Pays 3.
- Reward = base + (level - 1) for any earning result, x2 for veterans; failed = 0.
- Crash: collision that stops the car from above 40 px/s, 1 s grace.
- Failure/abort/quit returns the player on foot beside the giver with the car
  nearby; the same offer stays for a retry. Success gives a fresh offer.
- Result panel: detail, +mastery or "No mastery earned", level-up line.

ASSETS
marker-atlas icon_delivery, icon_speed, icon_drag, icon_*_hard, icon_dropoff,
icon_finish; people-atlas giver kinds (city_d, farmer_b, snow_a, nomad/explorer...);
track-atlas and vehicle-atlas racer_* for drag races (shared with 8).

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 2-world (positions, drivable destinations), 1-car (crashes), 6-progression
  (rewards, veterans, level-ups), 8-racing-centers (TrackLevel, Rival, calibration),
  5-ui (offer/result panels, HUD mission panel, guide to the target).
- Pause menu offers ABORT MISSION / QUIT RACE while one runs.

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/missions/missions.gd (autoload or child of Game): givers, offers
  (Dictionary per giver, saved), the active mission state machine.
- Delivery/time trial run in the world scene; drag races swap to the race scene
  from 8 and report back (signal race_finished).

DONE WHEN
- Offers show and persist across restarts; accepting starts the right mission.
- Delivery points drop per crash; tiers pay correctly; below 50 fails.
- Time trial limit matches the formula; running out fails.
- Drag race loads, counts down, and returns to the giver.
- Veterans unlock at level 5 and pay double; rewards scale with level.
