SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 6. PROGRESSION AND SAVES

PURPOSE
The player's growth: per-region mastery, levels, ratings, speed bonus, unlocks
(fast travel, veterans, fishing, island), the MAX cap, universal mastery points,
and what the save stores. Reference: progression.py, missions.py (unspent points,
spend), fast_travel.py, player_save.py, autosave.py.

HOW IT WORKS
- Regions: city, jungle, desert, snow, rural. Each has a level (starts 1), mastery
  toward the next level, completed missions by type, and center races won (0-10).
- Mastery to go from level L to L+1: 15 x L below level 10, 30 x L from 10 on.
- Rating = 100 + 10 x (level - 1) (level 1 = 100, level 51 = 600). Rivals are rated
  on the same scale; RATING_EDGE 10 sets Easy / Medium / Hard labels.
- Speed bonus: +4% top speed per level above 1 in that region (car speed_scale).
  The island (and beach) use the best region's level ("island" = best region).
- MAX_LEVEL 51: a region at 51 is MAX; it earns nothing more there, and a level-up
  that lands on 51 zeroes the leftover. Saves above 51 load as 51.
- Unlocks: level 3 in a region = fast travel there (TRAVEL on the Mastery page;
  lands in the car in the center's own lot); level 5 = veteran givers there;
  any region at level 4 = fishing, trading, and beach travel; every center 10/10 +
  a region at 25 = the Island pass can be used (see 15; keep this a surprise).
- Universal mastery points ("Mastery points" item): from fish trades, farm orders,
  stone shipments, tournaments. Held in the inventory (max 999), spent on any
  region from the spend menu (I -> Mastery points, or the Mastery page); a MAX
  region refuses them ("<Region> is MAX"). Level-ups can chain.
- Save contents (missions dict): progress {region: level, mastery, completed,
  races}, offer counter and per-giver offers, fish bag and caught log, unspent
  points, inventory items, arcade best scores and unlocks, farm, farm orders, fair,
  factory, island. Car/walker pose is saved but the game always opens at home.
  An ongoing mission is never saved (it waits at its giver).
- Autosave: every 60 s and right after mission results, purchases, race ends.

ASSETS
marker-atlas icon_points (mastery points), icon_token (tokens) for UI rows.

CONNECTIVITY
- Used by nearly everything: 1-car (speed_scale), 7-missions (rewards, veterans),
  8-racing-centers (ratings, races), 9-fishing/12-farm/14-factory/15-island (pay
  universal mastery), 5-ui (Mastery page, spend menu, level-up lines).
- Needs 0-foundation (Save/Autosave).

GODOT BUILD
- Autoload "Progress" (progress.gd): pure data + methods add(region, points),
  level, rating, speed_scale, is_max, fast_travel_ok, veterans_ok, fishing_ok,
  island_unlocked, to_dict/from_dict with validation.
- Autoload "Game" or "PlayerState": owns Progress, Inventory, and the other saved
  sub-states (farm, fair, factory, island...) and serializes them into Save.
- Spend menu scene (Control): choose a region, amount, confirm.

DONE WHEN
- Unit tests: mastery thresholds (15 x L / 30 x L), rating formula, +4%/level,
  the 51 cap (no overflow, leftover zeroed, saves clamped), unlock levels, spending
  refused on MAX, chained level-ups, and a full save/load round trip of every
  sub-state with bad data falling back to defaults.
