SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 1. CAR (DRIVING)

PURPOSE
The player's car: an arcade driving model whose grip and top speed depend on the
surface under it, plus handbrake, ice sliding, crashes, respawn, and the F1 upgrade.
Reference: car.py, collision_manager.py.

HOW IT WORKS
- Surfaces (grip, top speed px/s), from car.SURFACES:
    city 1.0/170   jungle 0.72/110   desert 0.78/132   snow 0.55/128
    rural 0.8/140  beach 0.68/105    island 0.9/155    track 1.0/170
  Off any known surface (race runoff): OFF_SURFACE = 0.75 grip, 110 px/s.
- Acceleration 85 px/s^2, braking 420, reverse acceleration 90 (all x grip).
  Reverse is capped at 80 x grip. TOP_SPEED 170 (city) scales the HUD bar.
- Speed scale: top speed x the region's level bonus (+4% per level above 1, from
  progression) x F1_SPEED 1.10 if the F1 car is owned (grip x F1_GRIP 1.20).
- Ice (snow surfaces): the car carries momentum sideways; ICE_TRACTION 1.8 and
  ICE_SCRUB 0.9 set how fast velocity turns toward the heading and bleeds off.
- Handbrake (Space while driving) for sharp turns.
- Crash: a hit that stops the car from above 40 px/s (CRASH_SPEED) counts (missions
  use it, with a 1 s grace).
- Collisions: the car's rotated box vs. solid scenery (buildings, props with solid
  sizes), traffic cars, parked cars, pedestrians (only when not already overlapping)
  and world bounds / sea. The car slides along what it hits.
- R (respawn): search outward rings (8 px apart, up to 480 px) for the nearest spot
  where the car plus 16 px clearance is drivable and clear; keep the heading, stop
  the car. Start position only if nothing nearby is free.
- Sprites: vehicle-atlas "racer_player" (or "racer_f1" once the F1 car is won),
  drawn 64 px.

ASSETS
vehicle-atlas: racer_player, racer_f1.

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs from 2-world: surface at a point (region_at / road_at), can_place_car(rect),
  nearby obstacles. Race tracks (8-racing-centers) and interiors provide the same
  interface, so the car works unchanged in every level.
- Needs from 6-progression: speed_scale(region). From 13-snow-fair: f1 flag.
- Feeds 5-ui (speed for the speedometer), 7-missions (crash events), 3-on-foot
  (the parked car becomes an obstacle).

GODOT BUILD
- scenes/car/Car.tscn: Node2D "Car" with a Sprite2D, script car.gd holding x, y,
  heading, speed, velocity; update(delta, throttle, steer, level, collisions,
  speed_scale). Do not use RigidBody2D: port the custom model so it feels the same.
- scripts/collision/collisions.gd: rotated-rectangle overlap tests against a local
  obstacle list from the level (grid-bucketed by sector). Optional: use
  PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D shape queries if the obstacles are StaticBody2D, but the
  "level" interface (region_at, can_place_car, nearby_obstacles) must stay.
- A Level interface (duck-typed or a base class) used by world, tracks, interiors.

DONE WHEN
- On a flat test level: 0 -> 170 px/s takes about 2.0 s on city; braking from 170
  takes about 0.42 s; each surface caps at its top speed.
- On ice the car visibly slides and recovers; on asphalt it doesn't.
- Driving into a wall stops the car and reports a crash above 40 px/s.
- R frees the car from inside a wall to the nearest clear spot.
- The F1 flag raises top speed 10% and grip 20% and swaps the sprite.
