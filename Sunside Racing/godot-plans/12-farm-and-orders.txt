SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 12. FARM AND FARM ORDERS

PURPOSE
The rural farm (claim, plot, animals) and the farm buyers who take orders across the
map. Reference: farm.py, farm_orders.py, main.py (_farm_door, _tend_plot,
_talk_to_buyer, _deliver_order).

HOW IT WORKS
- The farmhouse (rural region) stands abandoned (farmhouse_abandoned, boarded up)
  until rural level 6. E at its door then makes it the player's: +500 Sunside
  Tokens once. Before that E shows "Abandoned farmhouse" (the level needed is a
  mechanic and may be shown).
- Plot: a 5 x 5 mud plot (farm_mud) in front of the house. On a tile, E:
  empty -> plant (one seed; asks which when several kinds are owned), sprout ->
  fertilize (one super fertilizer makes it ripe at once), ripe -> harvest into the
  inventory (corn / tomato / lettuce). Refusals: no seeds, no fertilizer (price
  shown), can't carry more. Prompts: "E Plant a seed", "E Fertilize · Corn",
  "E Harvest · Corn". Plot state saved.
- Farmhouse interior (its own level, straw floor): 2 cows (Daisy, Clover) in stalls
  and 4 hens wandering the coop (34 px/s). E on a cow uses 1 cow feed -> 1 milk; E on
  a hen (within 52 px) uses 1 hen feed -> 1 egg. Look spots: nest boxes, hay. HUD
  panel shows feed and produce counts. Troughs, feeders, fence rails, hay piles.
- Farm orders: once the farm is owned, one buyer per mainland region, standing at
  one of that region's buyer spots from the fixed map export (map diamond "Farm
  buyer"); after a delivery the next buyer uses a different spot.
  - An order wants 1-3 kinds of goods (corn, tomato, lettuce, milk, eggs), 1-5 of
    each. Goods prices: corn 100, tomato 120, lettuce 140, milk 40, eggs 25.
  - Value = goods + 10% per km from the farm. Pays either Sunside Tokens (the
    value) or universal mastery (1 point per 20 S).
  - One active order at a time: talking to a buyer takes their order (TAKE
    ORDER / NOT NOW; switching keeps the old one open) and the guide arrow points
    at them. Bringing everything -> DELIVER. After delivery a new buyer appears
    elsewhere in that region. Refused if tokens or points are full.

ASSETS
structure-atlas farmhouse, farmhouse_abandoned, rural_barn; terrain-atlas farm_mud,
farm_field, farm_rows, floor_straw, wall*, dirt_gravel; farm-atlas (cow_a, cow_b,

store-atlas seeds_*/super_fertilizer/cow_feed/hen_feed icons; marker-atlas
icon_order; people-atlas buyer kinds (city_e, explorer_b, nomad_a, ...).

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 2-world (farm site, buyer spots per region), 6-progression (rural level 6,
  universal points), 10-inventory/store (seeds, fertilizer, feed; goods),
  5-ui (prompts, choice/confirm panels, O orders screen, map diamonds, guide).
- Feeds 14-factory (corn becomes biofuel).

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/farm/farm_state.gd (owned, 25 plot cells), scripts/farm/orders.gd
  (buyers, orders, active), scenes/interiors/Farmhouse.tscn (animals with simple
  wander AI), plot rendering as sprites on the world's farm site.

DONE WHEN
- Claim works only at rural 6 and pays 500 once.
- Plant -> fertilize -> harvest works per tile; saved across restarts.
- Feeding animals converts feed to milk/eggs.
- Orders price correctly (distance bonus, mastery rate), one active at a time,
  deliver pays and moves the buyer.
