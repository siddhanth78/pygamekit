SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 14. MINING FACTORY

PURPOSE
The desert-shore factory loop: corn -> biofuel -> stones -> shipped for tokens and
mastery at a moving stone market. Reference: factory.py, market_ui.py, main.py
(_factory_door, _depot, _dock, stone machine).

HOW IT WORKS
- Site (desert shore): factory building, a biofuel depot tank beside it with a
  pipe, and a cargo dock on the beach with a cargo ship. Map diamond "Mining
  Factory".
- Unlock: one Factory pass (20,000 S) used at the door, once, for good
  (UNLOCK / NOT NOW). Locked: "Entry needs a Factory pass."
- Depot (outside): corn -> biofuel 1:1 into a tank that holds 200; FILL UP takes as
  much corn as fits. The player never carries biofuel.
- Inside (factory floor level): the stone machine. E -> choose a run size:
  2 / 5 / 10 / 20 biofuel, each showing its odds (the card updates with the
  highlight):
    2:  80% iron, 20% copper      5:  80% copper, 20% silver
    10: 90% silver, 10% gold      20: 100% gold
  The run takes 3 s (conveyor, machine glow); the stone drops in the bin and goes
  to the inventory. Not enough biofuel -> refused.
- Stone market board (on the north wall): E opens a board with each stone's icon,
  today's price, its margin ("+10% over cost" green / "1% under cost" red), its cost
  and mastery, and a bar chart of the last 12 margins.
  - Cost of a stone = the biofuel of its floor run x 60 S (a corn = seed 10 +
    fertilizer 50): iron 120, copper 300, silver 600, gold 1,200.
  - Price = cost x (1 + margin), margin drawn in -10%..+20% for every stone after
    every shipment. Mastery fixed: iron 3, copper 5, silver 10, gold 25.
- Cargo dock: ships every stone carried at once: a bill (rows, total "x S + y
  mastery") with SHIP / NOT NOW, then a receipt; the market moves afterwards.
  Refused if tokens or mastery are full; "You have no stones to ship." when empty.
- HUD inside: "BIOFUEL n / 200" (or "MAKING A STONE · n BIOFUEL" and the seconds).

- Run odds match over many runs; runs need enough biofuel; 3 s timing.
- Prices = cost x (1 + margin) within range; the market moves only on a real

- Wired into the game flow as per plan 16 (interactions, prompts, mission locks, cleanup).
