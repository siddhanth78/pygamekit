SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 13. SNOW FAIR

PURPOSE
The Snow Fair: a fenced fairground in the snow region with six booth games, three
rides, food stands, crowds, and busy parking outside, plus its hidden grand prize.
Reference: fair.py, fair_games.py, fair_ui.py, main.py (_fair_gate, _interact_fair,
_update_fair_game, grand prize).

HOW IT WORKS
- Outside (world): a 2-sector block; a fenced mini fair (big top, ferris wheel,
  carousel) whose only way in is the ticket booth in the south fence; two rows of
  parking lots south of it joined to the snow road by an icy spur road; 4 visitor
  cars that come, park, and leave (90 px/s); 7 visitors walking lots <-> gate.
- Entry: E at the booth window costs 1 fair ticket (100 S, bought elsewhere); the
  prompt is "E   Snow Fair  ·  1 ticket". Confirm GO IN / NOT NOW.
- Inside (a level, city_plaza floor, snow edge): six booths in two rows, the game
  ticket counter by the gate (50 S each, packs of 1 / 5 / 10), three rides, food
  stands (popcorn, candy, food; one-line remarks), crowds. HUD panel: "GAME TICKETS
  n" and "Platinum booths n / 6".
- Every booth attempt and every ride costs 1 game ticket. Booth overlay: intro card
  (rules, "Your best", "Each band pays once.", four bands with pay) -> play ->
  result (score, band, tokens earned). ENTER plays again (1 ticket), ESC leaves.
  Walking away mid-attempt forfeits the ticket.
- Bands pay once each: bronze 10, silver 25, gold 50, platinum 150 S. Platinum is
  the maximum score (a flawless run). Booths stay open for fun after platinum.
- Games (field 480 x 360):
  - Darts: 3 darts; a figure-8 crosshair sweeps faster each dart; ENTER throws.
    Rings 50/25/20/15/10/5; bands 20/50/100/150.
  - High Striker: 3 swings; stop a bouncing meter; >= 95 rings the bell for 100,
    else the meter value. Bands 150/210/260/300.
  - Balloon Gallery: 10 pellets at balloons drifting in three rows (70, -105,
    140 px/s); arrows or mouse aim; a hit pops one. Bands 3/6/8/10.
  - Ring Toss: 5 rings; stop the side marker, then the distance marker; bottles in
    3 rows pay 30/20/10 (12 px reach). Bands 20/60/100/150.
  - Skee-Ball (slingshot): 6 balls; the ball slides along the lane (y 250, x
    40-440); ENTER or mouse press stops it; pull back and release (mouse drag
    relative to the grab, or arrows + ENTER) up to 100 px; it flies opposite the
    pull, 3 px per px, landing exactly where aimed; pulls under 8 px throw nothing.
    Corner holes at (70,42) and (410,42): touching one at all (hole 17 + ball 10 px)
    is 100 but only when stopped in that corner's quarter of the lane (x <= 120 /
    x >= 360, drawn as lighter wood); otherwise it bounces off the rim for 10 and
    rests below the hole. A scored corner ball sits in the hole's center. Rings
    (ellipses 2:1, radii 18/41/69/101) pay 50/40/30/20; elsewhere up the lane 10;
    short of y 175 rolls back for 0. Aim dots show the first half of the flight.
    Bands 100/180/280/600.
  - Whack-a-Mole: 30 s, 30 moles in a 3 x 3 grid, each up 1.2 -> 0.55 s; arrows +
    ENTER or click. Bands 10/18/25/30.
- Rides (1 ticket each, the camera zooms): Ferris wheel (8 cars, 16 s a lap, one
  lap), Carousel (6 horses, two turns), Bumper cars (20 s; drive with arrows in an
  arena; bumper cars within 46 px bump). The player sits in the ride and is put back
  where they got on.
- Grand prize (secret until it happens): all six booths at platinum -> "GRAND
  PRIZE" popup: the F1 car (+10% top speed, +20% grip everywhere, racer_f1 sprite)
  and Pit Stop on the home arcade. Never mention it before.

- Needs 2-world (site, spur road, lots), 4-traffic-and-people (visitor cars and
  walkers), 10-inventory (tickets, tokens), 3-on-foot, 5-ui.
- Feeds 1-car (f1 flag) and 11-arcade (Pit Stop unlock).

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/fair/fair_state.gd (best, band per game, f1), scripts/fair/games/*.gd
  (pure logic, one per game, shared interface update/press/point/click/release),
  scenes/fair/BoothOverlay.tscn (draws the field with _draw()), scenes/interiors/
  Fair.tscn (booths, rides as animated nodes, crowds), outside site in the world.

DONE WHEN
- A scripted flawless run reaches platinum in every game; one slip costs it.
- Skee-ball: rim touches count only from the right quarter; corner balls centered.
- Bands pay once; replays pay nothing new; booths stay open.
- The sixth platinum awards the F1 car and Pit Stop exactly once.
