SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 5. UI: HUD, PANELS, MENUS, MAP

PURPOSE
Everything drawn over the game: the HUD, the modal panel used by every interaction,
the pause menu (with the Mastery and Help pages), the title screen, the world map
with the guide arrow, and toasts. Reference: hud.py, mission_ui.py, pause_menu.py,
title_menu.py, world_map.py, ui_text.py, main.py (prompts, pending_* choices).

HOW IT WORKS
- HUD:
  - Top-left panel: place name and a guide line ("<Region> racing center · N m
    DIR", or the selected landmark, "Open water" at sea).
  - Speedometer bottom-right (248 x 64): KM/H number tinted by speed and a
    20-segment bar green -> yellow -> red scaled to TOP_SPEED; hidden on foot.
  - Top-right mission/info panel (title + one line), with a red crash flash.
  - Big center banner (countdown 3-2-1, GO!, YOU WIN / YOU LOSE).
  - Bottom-center prompt ("E   Get in", "E   Play Darts  ·  1 ticket"); a
    top-center "Saved" toast.
  - Guide arrow: yellow 32 x 16 arrow orbiting the player (56 -> 88 px) pointing at
    the selected landmark; hidden within 300 px of it or when none is selected.
- Modal panel (MissionPanel), one reusable scene with modes:
  - show_offer(title, difficulty, detail, rules, reward; ACCEPT / DECLINE).
  - show_result(success/failed, mastery, level up).
  - show_lines(title, chip, 3 lines, buttons) and show_message(title, line).
  - show_confirm(yes/no).
  - show_choice(title, lines, up to 4 options, optional per-option detail lines
    that follow the highlight).
  - show_bill(rows, total, note, buttons).
  - Chips: Success (green), Failed (red), Level up / Champion (gold),
    Island unlocked, Locked, ...
  - Keyboard (arrows + Enter/Space, Esc) and mouse. The game pauses while any
    panel is open; the caller learns the chosen button when it closes.
- Pause menu (Esc or window focus lost):
  - Buttons: RESUME, [ABORT MISSION / QUIT RACE when one is running], MASTERY,
    HELP, [GO HOME when nothing is running], EXIT.
  - Mastery page, one row per region: level, progress bar and mastery to next,
    speed bonus, veterans (Lvl 5 / Unlocked), completions, center x/10, ongoing
    mission, and TRAVEL (Lvl 3 / Here / Busy / TRAVEL). A status line under it
    stays empty until Elite Island opens.
  - Docks page for beach travel (see 9-fishing). Help page: controls table.
  - EXIT saves and quits.
- Title screen: a staged scene (the player's car on a city road, turned 45 deg)
  with SUNSIDE RACING and PLAY / EXIT. Nothing loads until PLAY.
- World map (M), 768 x 640 on the right, 12 x 10 px per sector; the left column has
  the MAP title, a legend, and the guide line with controls.
  - Regions are drawn as flat colors, with roads and the highway on top.
  - Landmark diamonds, color and label per kind: Racing center, Fishing pier, Fish
    trader camp, Veteran giver, General Store, Farmhouse, Farm buyer, Snow Fair,
    Mining Factory, Island ferry, Island club.
  - Click a diamond to set the guide, C clears it, M closes. Nothing is selected
    at the start.
  - The island center, club camps, and ferry diamonds appear only once unlocked or
    crossed (surprise rule).
- Text: every label auto-shrinks to fit. Wording rule from 0-foundation applies to
  every string.

ASSETS
None from the atlases except icons: marker-atlas (icon_* for panel icons if
wanted), vehicle-atlas racer_player (title scene), road/terrain tiles (title scene).
Panels, bars, diamonds, and arrows are flat shapes (StyleBoxFlat / Polygon2D).

CONNECTIVITY
- Every component opens the modal panel and sets the prompt; the panel reports the
  chosen option back (signal with the option id). Replace Python's pending_* flags
  with a callback/Callable passed to show_*().
- Needs 6-progression (Mastery page rows, travel), 2-world (map geometry, landmarks),
  7-missions (offers/results), 1-car (speed).

GODOT BUILD
- scenes/ui/HUD.tscn (CanvasLayer), ui/Panel.tscn (modal, one scene, modes as
  functions), ui/PauseMenu.tscn (pages as child Controls), ui/Title.tscn,
  ui/WorldMap.tscn (draws with _draw() onto a Control), ui/GuideArrow (Node2D in
  world space or drawn from the HUD with the camera transform).
- A UI stack manager: only the top overlay gets input; gameplay pauses while any
  overlay is open (get_tree().paused with process_mode on UI nodes).

DONE WHEN
- Every panel mode opens, navigates by keys and mouse, and returns the right choice.
- Pause menu buttons appear/disappear with mission/race state; TRAVEL works from
  the Mastery page; EXIT saves.
- The map shows regions and landmarks, sets and clears the guide; the arrow orbits
  and points correctly.
- Long strings shrink instead of clipping.
