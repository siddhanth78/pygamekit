SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 10. INVENTORY, GENERAL STORE, ORDERS VIEW

PURPOSE
The currency and item system, the inventory screen (I), the General Store interior
with its cart and checkout, and the orders screen (O). Reference: inventory.py,
inventory_ui.py, store.py, orders_ui.py, main.py (_interact_store, _pay_at_store).

HOW IT WORKS
- Inventory: a 7 x 4 grid of stacks (max 999; Sunside Tokens up to 999,999;
  one-time passes max 1). Sunside Tokens are permanent (always slot 1, even at 0).
  Items (id, name, store price, note — notes are one line, function only):
    sunside_tokens "The island's currency." | fish_common..epic "Trades for 1/2/3/5
    mastery." | mastery_points "Spend on any region." | seeds_corn 10, seeds_tomato 20,
    seeds_lettuce 30 | super_fertilizer 50 "Grows a sprout at once." | cow_feed 10 |
    hen_feed 10 | corn, tomato, lettuce, milk, eggs | fair_ticket 100 "Entry to the
    Snow Fair." | game_ticket "One fair game or ride." | stone_iron..gold |
    factory_pass 20,000 "Unlocks the factory." (1) | arcade_tow_train 10,000 (unlock,
    never carried) | tourney_pass 2,000 | island_pass 100,000 "Passage to the
    island." (1)
- Inventory screen (I): grid of icons with counts; arrows/mouse select; a details
  panel (name, one-line note); Mastery points opens the spend menu. Game pauses.
- General Store (a city building; walk in through its door): an interior level
  with a checkout counter by the door, a cashier, and three aisles (seeds, items,
  tickets) plus a game stand ("Game corner") with the Tow Train cartridge.
  E on a product puts one in the cart (refused if you can't carry it, already own a
  one-time item, or it's already in the cart). The cashier shows a bill (rows
  "name  n x price", total) with PAY / NOT YET; paying needs enough tokens; the
  receipt lists what was bought ("Unlocked: Tow Train." for the cartridge).
  Leaving with an unpaid cart asks to empty it (EMPTY CART / STAY).
  HUD panel: "CART · n items" and "total · you have x S". A few shoppers browse.
  Fast travel or GO HOME from inside puts the cart back on the shelves.
- Orders (O): every open farm order, nearest first: buyer, wants, pays, distance
  and direction, active/ready; ENTER works on an order (guide arrow to the buyer).
  "No orders yet." before any exist.

ASSETS
store-atlas (shelf, counter, ticket_board, crate, products seeds_*,
super_fertilizer, fair_ticket, factory_pass, island_pass, cow_feed, hen_feed,
store_mat, game_stand, arcade_tow_train), structure-atlas general_store, terrain
city_plaza/wall/wall_door/wall_window, people-atlas shoppers; item icons from their
atlases (farm-atlas crops, factory-atlas stones, fair-atlas game_ticket,
island-atlas tourney_pass, marker-atlas icon_token/icon_points, people-atlas fish_*).

CONNECTIVITY
- Inventory is used by every economy component (9, 12, 13, 14, 15, 11 arcade
  unlock). The store is the only seller of seeds, fertilizer, feed, passes, fair
  tickets, and the cartridge.
- Needs 6-progression (spend menu), 3-on-foot (interior walking), 5-ui (bill panel).

GODOT BUILD
- Autoload data: inventory.gd (items table as a Resource or Dictionary, add/remove
  with caps, contents for the grid). scenes/ui/Inventory.tscn, ui/Orders.tscn.
- scenes/interiors/Store.tscn (TileMapLayer floor/walls, fixtures, product spots as
  Area2D or distance checks), store.gd (cart, bill, pay).

DONE WHEN
- Caps and permanent tokens hold; one-time items refuse a second copy.
- A cart bill totals correctly; paying deducts tokens and adds items; the
  cartridge unlocks without entering the inventory; leaving asks about an unpaid
  cart.
- The inventory and orders screens navigate by keys and mouse.
