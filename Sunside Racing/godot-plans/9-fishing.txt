SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 9. FISHING AND FISH TRADERS

PURPOSE
Beach fishing from three piers, a timing-strike minigame, a fish bag, and fish
traders who pay universal mastery. Reference: fishing.py, fishing_ui.py (StrikeBar,
SpendMenu), world.py (DOCK_SITES, piers), pedestrians/world camps (traders).

HOW IT WORKS
- Opens when any region reaches level 4 (fishing, trading, and beach travel).
- Piers (west, north, east beaches), each 4 tiles out over the sea:
    Pier 1 west: any region at 4; mostly common (75/20/4.5/0.5 %).
    Pier 2 north: three regions at 7; more uncommon (40/45/14/1 %).
    Pier 3 east: every region at 12; more rare and epic (25/35/37/3 %).
  Locked piers show "Needs <who> at level <n>" (a mechanic, allowed).
- At a pier's end: E casts (bobber flies 150 px out, 0.5 s), wait 2 s, "FISH ON!"
  0.5 s, then strikes: a marker sweeps a bar; press SPACE while it's in the green.
  Strikes by rarity: common 2, uncommon 3, rare 4, epic 5; green zone 30/22/16/11 %
  of the bar; sweep speed 0.8/1.0/1.25/1.5 crossings per second; 4 s allowed per
  strike. A miss or timeout loses the fish. Then an automatic 1 s reel-in and the
  catch shows 2 s ("Caught: Rare Swordfish!", "Worth 3 mastery"). E casts again;
  moving away stops fishing.
- Fish names: common Sardine/Mackerel/Herring, uncommon Sea Bass/Red Snapper, rare
  Bluefin Tuna/Swordfish, epic Golden Marlin. Values 1/2/3/5 mastery.
- Bag: fish go to the inventory (fish_common..fish_epic). A full stack releases
  the catch.
- Fish traders: one beside the south tent of each jungle camp (fish_trader people
  sprite, floating icon_fishing badge). E trades the whole bag for universal
  mastery points (capped at 999: the rest waits).
- Beach travel: the pause menu's Docks page (and fast travel) parks the car on the
  sand at the chosen pier, facing inland.

ASSETS
terrain-atlas pier_planks, pier_end; people-atlas player_fish_cast,
player_fish_hold, player_fish_reel, fishing_bobber, fishing_bobber_bite,

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 2-world (piers, camps), 3-on-foot (walking onto piers; cars can't),
  6-progression (unlock levels, universal points), 10-inventory (bag and points),
  5-ui (strike bar overlay, Docks page, prompts "E Fish", "E Trade n fish").

GODOT BUILD
- scripts/fishing/fishing_session.gd (state machine: cast, wait, hooked, strike,
  between, reel, caught/escaped), scenes/ui/StrikeBar.tscn, trader interaction in
  the world scene.

DONE WHEN
- Locked/unlocked piers follow the level rules.
- A scripted perfect player lands every rarity; a miss or 4 s timeout loses it.
- Odds per pier match over many rolls; trading pays the right points and respects
  the cap.
