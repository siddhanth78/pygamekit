SUNSIDE RACING -> GODOT: 11. HOME AND ARCADE

PURPOSE
The player's house interior (living room and bedroom), its interactive furniture,
the badge board, and the arcade cabinet with three minigames. Reference: home.py,
arcade.py, badge_ui.py, main.py (_interact_home, _home_prompt).

HOW IT WORKS
- Outside: player_house on its lot in sector (21,31) with a two-stall lot where the
  game always starts (HOME_PARK, in the car). E at the front door goes in (not
  during a mission).
- Interior: 14 x 9 tiles (walls included); a dividing wall at column 9 with a
  doorway on rows 4-5; living room (wood floor) and bedroom (bedroom floor).
  Furniture drawn at 128 px (2x cells), solid where it has a footprint: rug, floor
  lamp, TV, coffee table, couch, arcade, bookshelf, dining table + 2 chairs, plants,
  bed, nightstand, wardrobe, and the white badge board on the bedroom's east wall.
- E spots: door (go outside), couch x2 and table chairs (sit: player_sit), bed
  (sleep: player_lie; HUD "Sleeping... Zzz"), lamps (toggle; lamp_on/lamp_off),
  TV / bookshelf / wardrobe / plant (one-line remarks), arcade (opens the cabinet),
  badge board (opens the badge board). Moving or E gets up.
- Badge board overlay: a white board on a dimmed screen with six badge slots as an
  inverted pyramid, 3 / 2 / 1 (bronze, silver, gold; green, blue; purple). Earned
  badges in color, the rest grey slots (badge_empty). No text. E/Enter/Esc/click
  closes. (Badges are earned in 15-elite-island.)
- Arcade cabinet: a menu of three games; best scores saved.
  - Lane Dodge (always): 3 lanes, cones fall from 220 px/s, +12 per cone dodged up
    to 720; LEFT/RIGHT switch lanes; one hit ends the run.
  - Pit Stop (locked until the Snow Fair grand prize): an arrow shows; press it
    within 1.3 s, the window shrinking x0.93 per call down to 0.38 s; a wrong or
    late key ends the run.
  - Tow Train (locked until bought): 12 x 16 yard of 30 px cells; the truck steps
    every 0.2 s, x0.96 faster per car collected down to 0.07 s; arrows steer (never
    straight back); each red car joins the chain; wall or own chain ends it.
  - Locked games show only "Unlocks later" (no hint how: surprise rule).

ASSETS
home-atlas (couch, tv, arcade, bed, lamp_off, lamp_on, dining_table, chair,
coffee_table, bookshelf, rug, plant, wardrobe, nightstand, badge_bronze..purple,
badge_empty, badge_board), terrain-atlas floor_wood, floor_bedroom, wall, wall_door,
wall_window, structure-atlas player_house, terrain home_lot. Arcade screens are
flat shapes; Lane Dodge can use vehicle-atlas racer_player and prop-atlas cone.

CONNECTIVITY
- Needs 3-on-foot (interior walking), 13-snow-fair (f1 -> Pit Stop), 10-store
  (arcade_unlocked -> Tow Train), 15-elite-island (badges), 6-saves (best scores).
- GO HOME (pause menu) and the spawn choice put the player here.

GODOT BUILD
- scenes/interiors/Home.tscn (TileMapLayer + furniture Sprite2Ds + spots),
  scenes/ui/BadgeBoard.tscn, scenes/arcade/Arcade.tscn with LaneDodge.gd,
  PitStop.gd, TowTrain.gd as pure logic + a drawing Control each.

DONE WHEN
- Every furniture spot does its action; sit/sleep poses and getting up work; lamps
  toggle.
- The badge board shows exactly the earned badges in the 3-2-1 layout.
- Each arcade game plays, ends by its rule, keeps the best score; locked games stay
  locked until their unlock.
